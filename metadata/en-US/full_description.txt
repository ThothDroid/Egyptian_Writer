Write ancient texts and browse all the avaible hieroglyphs on your tablet or phone.

This is a simple Android app for viewing, creating and exporting ancient Egyptian hieroglyphs. This app supports the professional writing of hieroglyphs using the Manuel de Codage. Hieroglyphs can be written in lines or columns, and both from left to right and from right to left.

This App is free and Open Source. Check out the Source Code: https://github.com/ThothDroid/Egyptian_Writer

Currently, the app supports five basic MdC code concepts
<ul>
<li><b>:</b> : To align signs vertically</li>
<li><b>*</b> : To align signs horizontally</li>
<li><b>()</b> : To group signs</li>
<li><b>.</b> and <b>..</b> : To make leave some space between the hieroglyphs
<li><b>!</b> and <b>!!</b> : To do a line break</li>
</ul>
